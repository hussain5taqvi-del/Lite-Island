Questions

Good to know

How much RAM does it use?

LiteIsland keeps itself at around 30 MB while it sits at the top of your screen. Heavy jobs like conversions and downloads run in separate helper processes and give their memory back when they finish.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

That message appears for every new app that is not signed with a paid certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Does it need internet?

No, except for the things that are online by nature: video downloads, the weather, wallpapers and updates. The converter, clipboard, music controls and Send to phone work offline.

Does Send to phone upload my files?

No. Files are never uploaded anywhere. Your phone downloads them directly from your PC over the same Wi-Fi, using a one-time random link that only the QR code contains. Closing the QR window stops sharing.

What about Mac and Linux?

Mac and Linux versions are in the works. Follow the page to know when they are out.
